## Board Quality Notes

- Built In: best signal in this pass. It surfaces Remote Poland roles and salary/location metadata better than most USA boards.
- Wellfound: good for startup/remote leads, but location eligibility must be verified carefully.
- CareerBuilder: some technical matches, but source quality is mixed; many roles may be agency or US-only.
- Dice: high volume but mostly US W2, onsite/hybrid, vendor submissions, in-person interview requirements, "no C2C", or US location constraints. Low priority for Poland-based applications.
- ZipRecruiter: useful for market scan but noisy; many results are US-only, on-site, estimated salary, or scraped from other sources.
- eFinancialCareers: useful for finance QA/SDET discovery, but many results are London/US/on-site or already represented elsewhere.
- PowerToFly: search surface exists, but this pass did not surface a strong current QA/SDET lead.
