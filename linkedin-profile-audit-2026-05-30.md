## Research-Based Best Practices Applied Here

The most useful LinkedIn profile advice for this case:

1. Use the target job titles directly in headline and Open to Work.
2. Put important recruiter-search keywords in multiple places: headline, About, Experience, Skills, Projects.
3. Use measurable achievements in Experience, not only responsibilities.
4. Attach skills to jobs, projects, certifications, and education.
5. Add Projects to demonstrate how skills were applied.
6. Keep About skimmable and front-load the strongest evidence.
7. Use Featured to show proof: portfolio, GitHub, CV, certifications, strong posts.
8. Get recommendations that prove the same positioning.
9. Keep activity aligned with target roles.
10. Avoid noisy or misleading signals near the top of the profile.

Important LinkedIn-specific note:

LinkedIn Skill Assessments are no longer available. LinkedIn's own guidance now points toward representing skills through profile evidence, especially jobs, projects, credentials, and education. That makes the empty Projects section and thin Experience descriptions more important to fix.
